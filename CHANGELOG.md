# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-11)

First public release.

- Case bands default to **By source**. The theme ships without anyone's shortcut list: every cover reads STEAM, and non-Steam shortcuts without artwork read APP. `tools/gen_sources.py` labels your own Heroic, emulator and app shortcuts, and can now run directly on the Deck.
- README, MIT licence and this changelog. Development notes moved to `docs/`.

## Before v1.0.0 (private testing)

- **v0.9.4:** "View more in your Library" sits on the same receding line as the games. Confirmation dialogs (Exit game, Uninstall, …) are themed.
- **v0.9.3:** The carousel's slide and depth move together in one motion (scroll-driven view timelines) instead of sliding first and correcting after.
- **v0.9.2:** Achievements: the summary folds away as the list scrolls, and the list fills the panel.
- **v0.9.1:** The in-game panel is themed. The folded Steam menu shows centred icons. Backgrounds reach the Steam menu and Quick Access windows.
- **v0.9:** Case bands by source: STEAM, HEROIC, RETRO and APP in matching colours.
- **v0.8–v0.8.1:** Downloads, Settings (and game Properties), Media and Friends & Chat. New "Other pages" option.
- **v0.7.3:** The Steam menu fits its window: no sideways shift, the accent tab sits inside the silver bar, and the gamercard band is gone.
- **v0.7–v0.7.2:**
  - game pages (new "Game page skin" option);
  - boxed context menus;
  - Steam's focus ring;
  - Community cards;
  - controller settings and View Layout;
  - a transparent top bar.
- **v0.6–v0.6.2:** The Library (new "Library skin" option) with a clean dusk sky. Quick Access buttons and focused rows are themed.
- **v0.5–v0.5.2:** The focused capsule is drawn in front. Home tab pages follow the selected tab. What's New sections are readable on the light panel.
- **v0.4:** Accent presets, with the sky following the accent. The Friends and Recommended tabs are readable.
- **v0.3:** NXE scrolling: the focused game is always left-most.
- **v0.2:** Rescaled for the Deck's 854×534 UI. NXE green background, plus Silver studio and Custom image. Carousel depth on by default.
- **v0.1:** First hardware test of the Home skin, Steam menu and Quick Access.
